# Surface brief: the descent (index.html)

Scope: the whole single-page experience. Mode: **Experience** (the visitor is inside the work; the artifact leads from the first viewport). Audience: first-time coders 13–21 and hackathon judges watching or clicking through. Action: type or pick a line of Python, then drill down through ten strata and surface again. Proof: every stratum is computed live from the visitor's code; reference checks (CPython, LLVM) are linked. Constraints: static, no key, LCP < 2.5 s, 375 px, WCAG 2.2 AA, reduced motion.

Candidates considered (ordered by resonance): 1 exploded-view instruction manual; 2 7400-series datasheet; 3 Powers of Ten film; **4 stratigraphic column / core sample (assigned by roll b8631867, degraded, no challengers)**; 5 subway line diagram; 6 engineering lab notebook; 7 punch cards / IBM design programme. Rut refused: dark IDE with neon syntax colours; its opposite, a pastel cartoon textbook.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A computer is sedimentary. Your line of code is the newest deposit, sitting on strata laid down since 1937. The page is a core sample drilled straight down through them. It refuses the category default of a dark IDE full of neon code, and its opposite, a friendly pastel textbook.

OWN-WORLD: Geological survey publication. Each stratum owns a full-width field in its official ICS/CGMW period colour (Quaternary #F9F97F at the surface down to Devonian #CB8C37), shown as a light tint for reading and full strength in its lithology pattern (dots, dashes, brick, crosses as SVG). A persistent borehole-log column on the left shows every band. Type: Anybody (display; its width axis *compresses with depth*, as sediment does), Public Sans (body, the US government publication voice), Fragment Mono (code and measurements only). Hairline rules and square corners. Ink text, never grey on colour.

STORY: The visitor types one line, presses Drill, and falls through letters, bytes, tokens, tree, bytecode, VM, machine code, gates and transistors, each rendering *their* program. They surface at a dark screen where their output lights up pixel by pixel. They leave able to name every layer, and believing it was all real.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, 64 px: the full borehole log (ten coloured bands with patterns, depth ticks, a marker at 0). Main, on the Quaternary field: the headline "Type one line of Python. Follow it all the way down." in Anybody at wdth 110, around 88 px, left-aligned, two lines. Below it, a large Fragment Mono editor pre-filled with `print("hi " * 3)` and a caret, preset chips beneath. Primary button **Drill** (with ↓), secondary **Watch the dive**. At the bottom edge, 12 vh of the Neogene band already rising, showing the code's characters falling into bytes.

FORM: Stratigraphic column / core sample; my list position 4; seed key b8631867 (degraded roll).

Signature interaction: **the drill**. Scroll, press ↓/J, or click a band in the log: the page descends stratum by stratum, the log marker sinks, the display width compresses, and each stratum's visual transforms the one above it (characters fan into bytes, tokens snap, the tree grows downward, bytecode stacks, carries ripple right-to-left). **Watch the dive** runs the whole descent in about 30 seconds. Reduced motion makes every change instant.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
