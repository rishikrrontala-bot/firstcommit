## Pitch, verbatim (README)
> LoreLeaf is a cozy fantasy study room web app built for students and made by students. The goal of the project is to help users stay focused, complete study sessions, and feel rewarded through a shared magical tree that grows as users earn focus points through doing their tasks.

Tagline: *"The Library that Never Sleeps."*

## The wow moment
A shared magical tree in a hand-illustrated fantasy library that visibly grows through five art stages as everyone's focus minutes accumulate.

- Working per README: Pomodoro timer with a clear points formula, shared tree with 5 stages, progress display, room UI.

## Stack
Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind, Vercel. The stack isn't the story; the **art direction** is (custom tree illustrations, medieval-cozy palette).

## Submission page shape
README: badges, a 1920×1080 hero image, then Project Theme → Problem → Core Features (with exact point rules and a worked example) → a tree-stage table with images.

## Why this won (one sentence)
A common idea (a Pomodoro timer) won Best Design twice because it committed to one coherent, hand-made world and explained its rules precisely.

## Transferable to us
- **Copy:** one committed art direction carried through every screen; exact, worked-example explanations ("a 45-minute session earns 34 points"); a hero image at the top of the README.
- **Don't copy:** study-timer territory (crowded in this event already: FocusStudy is entered here), or backend-dependent shared state that can fail on a cold judge visit.
