## Pitch, verbatim (README "Inspiration")
> MIX&MATCH! was born out of a feeling most teenage girls experience... the exhausting, mental struggle between who you are and who the world wants you to be. We wanted to take a genre that's always been about fun and freedom (the dress-up game) and turn it into something that actually says something.

Tagline: *"A totally normal dress-up game... or is it?"*

## The wow moment
The meta-horror turn: a cheerful dress-up game begins to push back on the player's choices (in the style of *Doki Doki Literature Club*).

## Demo teardown
- Not checked (YouTube blocked). The README embeds the video thumbnail as its first element, above everything else.

## Scope reality
- Working: drag-and-drop clothing with snap zones, character creator that persists name and skin tone, typewriter terminal text, two branching endings, hand-drawn cutscene, 80+ outfit combinations.
- Described as cut: extra rounds, more customisation, a longer cutscene ("We had to cut several ideas…").
- Commit window: 5 commits, Mar 21–25, 2026. Two builders across Egypt and India.

## Stack
Godot (GDScript), Krita/Procreate, Canva. The *hand-drawn art* is the story, not the engine.

## Submission page shape
README follows the Devpost template exactly: Inspiration → What It Does → How We Built It → Challenges → Accomplishments → What's Next → Built With. The Challenges and Accomplishments sections are candid and specific ("the drag-and-drop system — only took 10 hours of work and debugging ;)").

## Why this won (one sentence)
A genre subversion with a personal message, and an honest account of what the builders learned and what they cut.

## Transferable to us
- **Copy:** candid, specific Challenges and Accomplishments sections (exactly what FirstCommit's Learning & Growth criterion rewards); the video first in the README; a twist announced in the tagline.
