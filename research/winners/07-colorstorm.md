## Pitch, verbatim (README)
> Pick a color amidst the storm. The flood pushes the insignificant away.

Tagline: *"Color picker meets the Storm from Fortnite, Kahoot, and Osu."*

## The wow moment
The shrinking "storm": each correct pick narrows the gallery until the remaining colors are nearly indistinguishable. Your own perception becomes the difficulty curve.
