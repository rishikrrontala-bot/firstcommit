## Pitch, verbatim (README)
> Micro-volunteering, one swipe at a time. GiveFive turns volunteering into 5-minute tasks instead of hour-long commitments — proofread a paragraph, explain a confusing form, do a quick practice interview, or just keep someone company on a call.

## The wow moment
A familiar gesture (a dating-app swipe) repurposed for kindness: swipe right to commit five minutes of help.

## Demo teardown

- Working: swipe deck (drag or buttons), category filter, undo, commitments list, impact dashboard (minutes, people, streak, badges), installable PWA.

## Stack
React, Vite, lucide-react, a service worker (PWA).

## Submission page shape
README: one-line hook, a paragraph of concrete examples, feature bullets, a stack line with an honest limitation, beginner-proof setup steps, "install as an app" instructions, file tree, demo.

## Why this won (one sentence)
A borrowed interaction pattern (the swipe) makes a worthy idea feel fun, and the design commits to it.

## Transferable to us
- **Copy:** state the limitation plainly ("No backend… in-memory"), which buys credibility; concrete examples in the first paragraph; setup steps a first-timer can follow.
- **Don't copy:** fake impact numbers in a dashboard with no real users behind them.
